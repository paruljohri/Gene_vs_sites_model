These scripts all make the final tables for LD and load statistics. Descriptions of the R scripts:

LD STATISTICS
-make_table_LD_stats_multiple_sites_v2.r
Obtains the mean and SEs of LD statistics across the independent simulation replicates when there is no epistasis. Summarizes these values between selected as well as minor alleles.

-make_table_LD_stats_multiple_sites_v2_N5000.r
Obtains the mean and SEs of LD statistics across the independent simulation replicates when there is no epistasis and when 5000 diploid individuals are used to perform simulations (as opposed to 1000 in most cases). Summarizes these values between selected as well as minor alleles.

-make_table_LD_stats_multiple_sites_v2_humans.r
Obtains the mean and SEs of LD statistics across the independent simulation replicates when there is no epistasis for human-like simulations. Summarizes these values between selected as well as minor alleles.

-make_table_LD_stats_multiple_sites_v2_multiplicative.r
Obtains the mean and SEs of LD statistics across the independent simulation replicates when there is no epistasis and fitness is multiplicative. Summarizes these values between selected as well as minor alleles.

-make_table_LD_stats_multiple_sites_epistasis_v2.r
Obtains the mean and SEs of LD statistics across the independent simulation replicates when there is epistasis. Summarizes these values between selected as well as minor alleles.
